Comprehensive Environmental Laboratory Services

Our ISO-compliant laboratory services provide accurate, science-backed testing and analysis for a wide range of environmental samples, including air, water, soil, effluents, and hazardous materials. We combine advanced instrumentation with rigorous quality control to deliver reliable data that supports regulatory compliance, risk assessments, and sustainable decision-making.

With expertise across multiple sectors, including oil & gas, construction, and manufacturing, we help clients identify potential risks early, meet environmental standards, and maintain operational excellence.

  • ISO/IEC 17025-certified testing protocols

  • Air quality analysis and emissions monitoring

  • Water and wastewater quality testing

  • Soil and sediment contamination studies

  • Effluent characterization and treatment recommendations

  • Hazardous waste identification and profiling

  • Heavy metals, hydrocarbons, and microbiological testing

  • Regulatory reporting and compliance documentation
