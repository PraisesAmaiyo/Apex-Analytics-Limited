Environmental Evaluation Study / Environmental Audit

Apex Analytics Limited partnered with SPDC-Winorac (2024–2025) to deliver a comprehensive Environmental Evaluation Study and Environmental Audit for the Nun River Flowstation. The requirement was to conduct a thorough assessment of environmental conditions, compliance status, and operational impacts, ensuring sustainability and adherence to statutory guidelines.

Our team executed a structured audit process, integrating baseline data review, site inspections, and stakeholder engagement. By applying international best practices, we provided a clear evaluation of environmental risks and actionable recommendations to improve compliance and performance.

Project Highlights

01. Two-Season Data Gathering

We carried out extensive data collection over two seasons, capturing wet and dry period variations. This approach ensured a complete understanding of environmental conditions around the Nun River Flowstation, supporting accurate compliance evaluation.

02. Comprehensive Environmental Audit

Our audit combined field measurements, laboratory analysis, and regulatory benchmarking to assess potential risks, evaluate mitigation strategies, and recommend improvements for sustainable operations.

    Key Deliverables

    Our approach to the Nun River Flowstation project was grounded in precision, compliance, and sustainability. Each deliverable was structured to align with both client requirements and regulatory expectations.

  • Seasonal Data Gathering – Wet and dry season datasets for accurate baseline assessment

  • Full Environmental Audit – Review of compliance with national and international standards

  • Risk Identification & Mitigation – Practical solutions for minimizing operational impacts

  • Actionable Recommendations – Guidance for sustainable environmental management

Conclusion

This project highlights Apex Analytics Limited’s expertise in conducting Environmental Evaluation Studies and Audits that meet rigorous compliance standards. Through two-season data gathering and detailed reporting, we equipped SPDC-Winorac with the insights needed to maintain regulatory alignment, safeguard the environment, and enhance operational sustainability.
