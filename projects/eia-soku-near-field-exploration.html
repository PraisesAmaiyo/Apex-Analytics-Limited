Environmental Impact Assessment – Soku Near Field Exploration

In 2023, Apex Analytics Limited was engaged by SPDC-Winorac to carry out an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) for the proposed Soku Near Field Exploration Wells Project. The requirement was to ensure all exploration activities aligned with environmental regulations, community safety standards, and sustainable development objectives.

Our team delivered a One Season Data Gathering and Reporting solution. This involved comprehensive environmental baseline studies, regulatory reviews, and stakeholder consultations to provide clear, evidence-based recommendations for project approval and sustainable execution.

Project Highlights

01. One-Season Data Collection

We executed a complete season of data gathering, including ecological surveys, water and soil sampling, and air quality monitoring. This ensured the project captured an accurate environmental profile of the proposed site within the stipulated timeframe.

02. Regulatory-Compliant Reporting

The gathered data was analyzed and compiled into a structured Environmental Impact Assessment report, aligned with Nigerian regulatory requirements and international best practices, enabling smooth review and approval processes.

    Key Deliverables

    Our EIA approach for the Soku Near Field Exploration Wells Project was built on precision, sustainability, and compliance. The project output provided SPDC-Winorac with actionable insights for environmentally responsible exploration.

  • One-season baseline environmental study – capturing ecological, hydrological, and atmospheric data.

  • Regulatory-compliant EIA reporting – aligned with Nigerian environmental regulations and standards.

  • Stakeholder engagement – consultations with host communities and regulators for inclusive decision-making.

  • Actionable recommendations – clear strategies to mitigate environmental risks and support sustainable exploration.

Conclusion

This project highlights Apex Analytics Limited’s expertise in delivering regulatory-compliant and data-driven environmental solutions. By providing SPDC-Winorac with accurate baseline data and actionable insights, we supported safe exploration activities that protect both local communities and the surrounding ecosystem.
