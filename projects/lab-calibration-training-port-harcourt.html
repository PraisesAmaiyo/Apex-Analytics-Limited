Laboratory Calibration & Training Service - EFCC, Port Harcourt

In 2024, Apex Analytics Limited was engaged by the EFCC to provide specialized laboratory calibration and training support for its Port Harcourt Office. The requirement focused on ensuring the accuracy and reliability of laboratory equipment while simultaneously strengthening the technical competence of in-house staff.

Our team combined hands-on calibration expertise with tailored staff training modules, creating a sustainable framework for improved laboratory operations. This dual approach enhanced equipment precision, reduced downtime, and empowered personnel with practical knowledge for future operations.

Project Highlights

01. Comprehensive Equipment Calibration

Our experts executed ISO-standard calibration of key laboratory instruments to ensure compliance with national and international quality benchmarks. This process minimized measurement errors and improved reliability in daily operations.

02. Capacity-Building Through Training

We developed and delivered hands-on training sessions for EFCC laboratory staff, focusing on calibration procedures, routine equipment checks, and best practices in laboratory management for long-term efficiency.

    Key Deliverables

    The Port Harcourt calibration and training engagement was structured to combine technical excellence with knowledge transfer. Every stage of the project emphasized precision, compliance, and sustainability.

  • ISO-Compliant Calibration – Standardized testing and adjustment of instruments

  • Staff Training & Knowledge Transfer – Hands-on modules for sustainable operations

  • Operational Efficiency – Reduced equipment downtime and improved accuracy

  • Compliance Assurance – Alignment with Nigerian and international laboratory standards

Conclusion

This project highlights Apex Analytics Limited’s ability to deliver not only technical calibration expertise but also long-term value through staff empowerment. By integrating precision equipment services with training, we enabled EFCC’s Port Harcourt laboratory to achieve higher operational standards, compliance, and sustainability.
