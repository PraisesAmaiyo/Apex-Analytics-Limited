EIA of Proposed Obodo Appraisal Drilling and Surface Facilities in OML 150

In 2024, Apex Analytics Limited supported CONOG with the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) of the proposed Obodo Appraisal Drilling and Surface Facilities in OML 150. The project required a comprehensive evaluation of the potential environmental and community risks associated with the drilling operations and facility development.

Our team deployed multidisciplinary expertise in environmental science, modelling, and field data acquisition. By integrating one-season baseline data gathering, air dispersion modelling, and technical reporting, we provided CONOG with a regulatory-compliant framework that enabled safe and sustainable project execution.

Project Highlights

01. One-Season Baseline Study

Our specialists conducted seasonal field data collection covering air, water, soil, and ecological indicators. This ensured a complete environmental profile representative of the operational area in OML 150.

02. Advanced Air Dispersion Modelling

Using industry-standard modelling tools, we simulated the potential impact of emissions from drilling and surface facilities. This analysis provided reliable predictions to guide mitigation planning and community protection measures.

    Key Deliverables

    Our delivery approach was built on compliance, accuracy, and actionable insights. Each phase of the EIA process was aligned with Nigerian regulatory frameworks and international best practices.

  • Comprehensive Seasonal Data Gathering – air, water, soil, and ecological parameters

  • Air Dispersion Modelling – predictive analysis of emissions and their potential impacts

  • Regulatory-Ready EIA Report – documentation aligned with Nigerian law and international standards

  • Actionable Mitigation Strategies – practical recommendations for sustainable drilling and facility operations

Conclusion

This project highlights Apex Analytics Limited’s capacity to deliver world-class Environmental Impact Assessments. Through rigorous fieldwork, scientific modelling, and compliance-focused reporting, we enabled CONOG to advance its operations in OML 150 with confidence—ensuring both environmental stewardship and sustainable development outcomes.
